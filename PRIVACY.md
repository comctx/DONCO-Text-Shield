# DONCO Text Shield privacy and help

## What this app does

Text Shield applies phrase rules on your iPhone to messages Apple sends to its filtering extension. It recommends a category to Messages. It does not authenticate the sender, guarantee safety, delete messages, or read your entire inbox.

## Messages and sample text

Text Shield does not upload message content, keep a message history, or use a remote classification service. The extension processes eligible message text in memory. Text typed into the sample tool is processed locally and is not intentionally saved; it is cleared when the tool closes or the app leaves the foreground.

## Saved choices

Only your filter switches are stored by this app in shared iPhone preferences so the app and extension can use them. No account, advertising SDK, analytics service, tracking identifier, or cloud-sync feature is included. iOS may manage device backups and diagnostics under your Apple settings; this app does not control Apple's services.

## Filtering can make mistakes

Legitimate messages can be filtered and scams can pass. Codes, bank alerts, appointments, and voting information are not guaranteed to remain visible. Check filtered folders regularly, especially when expecting an important message. Rules are primarily English phrases and may miss other wording or languages.

## Find a missing message

In Messages, open Filters and check Junk or Spam, Promotions, and Unknown Senders. Folder names and recovery controls vary by iOS version. If offered for a message you recognize, use Not Spam or Mark as Known. Check the sender through a trusted source before opening links or sharing information. Text Shield cannot restore or move messages itself.

## Pause filtering

Use Turn all filtering off on the main screen to save all four filter categories as off. This affects Text Shield's decisions only; Apple or carrier filtering can still apply. To disable the extension, open the message filtering controls in iPhone Settings and turn off or deselect DONCO Text Shield. Activation and actual settings sharing must be checked on your device.

## Settings problems

If settings are missing, unreadable, or unavailable, this version allows messages through instead of turning filters on. The app warns when a setting change cannot be verified. A successful preference read-back does not prove that the extension has received the change.

## Help and privacy questions

The project issue page is a public support channel hosted by GitHub. Visiting it opens an external service with its own privacy practices; creating an issue requires GitHub access. Do not post real messages, phone numbers, passwords, verification codes, financial information, or other private details. Use a made-up example instead.
